## 🧬 About Me

Hey there! I'm **Sushant** — a bioinformatician obsessed with turning raw sequencing data into meaningful biological insights. I live at the intersection of **biology, data, and code**, building pipelines that are clean, modular, and actually reproducible (yes, really).

- 🔭 Specializing in **Genomics**, with deep expertise in Transcriptomics, Epigenomics & Metagenomics
- ⚙️ Passionate about **modular pipeline design** and **scientific reproducibility**
- 🖥️ Experienced working with **HPC servers** and messy, real-world biological datasets
